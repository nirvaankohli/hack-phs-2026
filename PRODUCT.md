# Cloudbound product

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
Hackathon players and judges playing a short, approachable flying game on a laptop. Capture a face, attach angel wings, and survive a flight into the sky. Physical wing tracking and keyboard input share flap, glide, and dive states.

## Personality and principles
Playful, airy, expressive. Follow the approved Cloudbound concept and supplied pixel-art references. The player's face is the hero. Readable obstacles, fair collisions, instant retries, and a visibly decorative corner sun take priority over complexity. Avoid busy playfields and unrelated hazards.

## Accessibility
Keyboard navigation, keyboard fallback, mute, reduced ambient motion, readable controls, and explicit flight-state labels. Tracking loss pauses the run.
