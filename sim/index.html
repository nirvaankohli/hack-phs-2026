Skyborne

You are a bald eagle above an endless coast. Ride thermals under the cumulus clouds and the updrafts on windward ridges, stoop into a dive, and land wherever you like.

Wing controllerRun python tracking/wing_controller.py --sim (it opens this page), hold the wing level and press F8. The eagle copies your arms: tilt to bank, hold the wing high to climb or low to dive, and beat down to flap and gain height.
Keyboard← → / A D bank · ↑ ↓ / W S tilt the flight angle up or down (it stays where you leave it; I inverts) · Space flap (hold to keep flapping)
GamepadLeft stick to fly, A to flap. Works with --gamepad.
SoaringThe climb meter and beeping mean rising air. Circle tightly inside it to gain height. Flapping uses stamina; gliding restores it.
