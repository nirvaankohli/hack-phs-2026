# Cloudbound design

A player stands at a laptop in a bright hackathon room, smiling at their face flying through an inviting pixel sky. Use a light sky, dark teal ink, warm cream panels, and golden primary actions. The supplied blue sky sets the palette. Use the exact supplied sun, wings, birds, and gray storm-cloud sprites. Use Pixelify Sans for every heading, label, input, button, and HUD readout. Square pixel-style buttons have visible focus. The start screen contains only Cloudbound, Play, and Settings, with no slogans or extra copy. Keep the webcam out of the playfield.
